## Findings

**Minor observations (not defects, behavior is correct):**

2. **File: `packages/core/src/state/opHistory.ts:13-23`** - `buildOpHistory` computes `concurrentWith` for all ops including deleted records, which is correct per R2.5 ("including those of a deleted record").
